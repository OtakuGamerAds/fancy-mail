# Privacy policy

Fancy Mail is a desktop email app that runs on your own computer. There is no Fancy Mail server
and no Fancy Mail account.

## What it reads

When you add an account, Fancy Mail connects straight from your computer to your mail provider
(for Gmail, Google's IMAP and SMTP servers) to download, show, search, organize and send your
mail. When you sign in with Google or Microsoft, it receives an access token for that, and it
uses that token for nothing else.

## Where it is kept

Your mail, settings and sign-in tokens are stored only on your computer, in the app's data folder.
Passwords and tokens are encrypted there. Removing an account deletes its mail and tokens
from your computer.

## What is shared

Nothing is sent to the developers of Fancy Mail or to anyone else. The app has no analytics,
no tracking and no ads, and it does not sell or transfer your data. The only other connection
it makes is to GitHub, to check for new versions of the app. Images that an email loads from
its sender's server are blocked until you choose to show them.

If you connect an AI agent or another tool to Fancy Mail's local MCP server, that tool can read
the accounts you allow it to read. That happens only when you set it up, and the tool's own
privacy terms apply to what it does with the data.

## Google user data

Fancy Mail's use of information received from Google APIs follows the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking access

You can remove an account in Fancy Mail at any time. To also revoke the app's access on Google's
side, visit [your Google Account's connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
